About Färjestad BK

Train with coaches from Färjestad BK, one of Sweden's most successful hockey clubs! Färjestad competes in the Swedish Hockey League (SHL) — the top professional league in Sweden — and were regular season champions last year. Their experienced coaching staff has worked with superstar NHL players and SHL professionals, bringing world-class knowledge and techniques straight to you.

Meet the Team of Coaches!

Niclas Glader

Niclas is a pro skills coach with many years of experience coaching players, all from NHL-superstars to the youngest players. His main area of coaching is power skating and stick handling.

Linnea Persson

Linnea is our strength and conditioning coach and has as well as years of experience working in our camps also a degree in sports science.

Alexander Olsson

Alex has had a career as a goalie in both Sweden U20 elite league and in North America. He now coaches Färjestad's U15 goalies and works on Färjestad's camps.

Tobias Nilsson

Tobias has grown up and played in Färjestad BK's organization during his junior years. He has now switched over to the coaching side and besides being a U15 head coach also works at all of Färjestad's Camps. Tobias main area is game understanding and stick handling.

Joakim Hedlund

Joakim is one of our own coaches with lots of experience in coaching teams on different levels, such as skills coaching on our camps. His main area is power skating and stick handling.

What to Expect

Each day is packed with high-level training and fun:

  • 3 on-ice sessions per day focusing on skating, puck control, and game sense
  • Daily off-ice stickhandling and shooting drills
  • Mobility, strength, and conditioning sessions to boost your performance
  • Lunch included to keep you fuelled and ready to play

Camp Fee

Family discounts:

  • 3 kids: €200,- discount from the total price
